Write 8 short questions that place them, from the easiest to the hardest: one of level A1, two of A2, two of B1, two of B2 and one of C1 (Common European Framework of Reference). Each is one sentence in $learned with one gap, written ____ (four underscores), and four choices in $learned to fill it: one only is right, and telling it from the others takes exactly the grammar or the vocabulary of that level.

- level: A1, A2, B1, B2 or C1.
- sentence: the sentence with its gap, 16 words at most.
- choices: four different words or short phrases.
- answer: the index (from 0) of the right one.
